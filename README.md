## WAYPETS - Pet Shop Script

> More than just a pet...

### Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Name Your Pet** | Choose the name you love for your pet |
| **Follow & Companion** | Your pet follows you, enters interiors and stays with you |
| **Play With Your Pet** | Interact, play and have fun together |
| **Pet Vehicle System** | Your pet rides with you in the car. Enter & exit with special interactions |
| **Hunger System** | Feed your pet from food in your inventory. Special eating animation |
| **Happiness System** | Your pet's happiness increases with care, play and food |
| **Pet Behavior** | Sit, stand, sleep, interact and more. Cats sleep when you sit |

### Pet Shop
- Beautiful and organized shop with detailed information
- Dogs, cats and more. Choose your favorite
- Prices example: Golden Retriever $2,500 | British Shorthair $2,000 | Husky $2,500

### Owned Pets
- Your pets are saved
- Call them anytime
- Support multiple pets

### Multi-Language
Available in:
- Arabic (/catar)
- English (/caten)
- French (/catfr)
- Chinese (/catchi)

### Key Commands
```
/dog      → Call your dog
/cat      → Call your cat
/dogoff   → Remove dog
/catoff   → Remove cat
/animspawn → Spawn pet (small)
```
